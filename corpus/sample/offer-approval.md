title: Offer approval workflow
id: offer-approval
category: workflow
---

All offers require sequential approval before release to the candidate.

Step 1: Hiring manager submits offer packet in the HRIS with comp band justification.
Step 2: Finance validates band and equity pool availability.
Step 3: People Ops checks start date, location, and compliance flags.
Step 4: Regional HRBP gives final approval for director+ roles.

Do not send verbal offers before Step 4 completes. Use the standard offer letter template only.

Escalation for stuck approvals: People Ops queue owner (see people directory).
