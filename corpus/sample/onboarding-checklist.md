---
title: New hire onboarding checklist
id: onboarding-checklist
category: procedure
---

New hires at Acme Corp complete onboarding within their first five business days.

Day 1: IT provisions laptop and SSO. HR sends the welcome packet and I-9 window.
Day 2: Manager runs the 30-minute team intro and assigns a onboarding buddy.
Day 3: Complete mandatory security training in the learning portal.
Day 4: Benefits enrollment deadline — use the HR portal, not email forms.
Day 5: People Ops confirms checklist completion in the HRIS.

Escalation: if SSO or laptop is blocked, contact IT Service Desk (it-help@acme.example).
