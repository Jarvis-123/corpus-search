---
title: Remote work policy
id: remote-work-policy
category: policy
---

Acme Corp supports hybrid work: minimum two days per week in an approved office unless role is marked Remote.

Remote-only roles require VP approval and a documented timezone overlap plan.
Employees must update their work location in the HRIS when relocating across states or countries.

Cross-border work without legal review is not allowed. Contact People Ops before travel longer than 30 days.

Equipment: company laptop only for company data. Personal devices require MDM enrollment.
