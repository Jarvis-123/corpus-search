id: headcount-reporting

---

Live headcount and open req counts live in the HRIS analytics module — not in this handbook.

People Ops publishes a weekly snapshot every Monday 09:00 UTC to leaders.
Do not copy numbers into slide decks without checking the HRIS export timestamp.

For custom reports, request access via people-ops@acme.example with business justification.

This handbook does not store live metrics.
