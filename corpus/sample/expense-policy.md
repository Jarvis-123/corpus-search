---
title: Expense reimbursement policy
id: expense-policy
category: policy
---

Employees may submit expenses within 30 days of incurring the cost.

Allowed without pre-approval: client meals under $75, local travel under $50, software under $25/month.
Requires manager approval: any single expense over $150, international travel, hardware purchases.
Not reimbursable: personal subscriptions, alcohol without client present, fines or penalties.

Submit receipts via the Finance portal. Reimbursement SLA is 10 business days after approval.

Violations should be reported to Finance Compliance (finance-compliance@acme.example).
